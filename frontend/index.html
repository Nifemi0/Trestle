ENROLLED CHAINS
    SETTLEMENT

    Four steps, no custody.

    Your signature moves the token on the source chain. Everything after that is the message passing itself.

    01

    Sign on the source chain

    Approve and sign transferRemote from your wallet. Funds leave your balance only when you confirm.

    02

    The mailbox records it

    The router locks the token and posts a message to the Hyperlane mailbox with your address and amount inside.

    03

    The relayer carries it

    An always-on process watches every enrolled mailbox and delivers the message to the destination chain within seconds.

    04

    Delivered to your wallet

    The destination router mints the mesh token to the same address. Your balance updates on its own.
